Your Miami Yacht Experience Starts Here

Scroll to discover

Miami Yacht Collective started with one frustration: booking a yacht in Miami had become a circus of platforms, brokers, and service fees stacked on service fees. You’d send an inquiry on Friday night and hear back Monday afternoon — by which point the date was already gone.

So we built something simpler. A small, hand-picked fleet — from a 25′ Yamaha jet boat to a 90′ superyacht — out of one dock on the Miami River. When you call, you talk directly to the team that handles your charter from inquiry to dock-back.

Our captains know Biscayne Bay the way most people know their neighborhood. Star Island, Nixon’s Sandbar, Stiltsville — routed however the day calls for it. The boats are clean, the crew shows up early, and the music gets loud only when you want it to.

How booking works

Step 1

Call or WhatsApp

Tell us your date, group size and the occasion. We answer 24/7 and check live availability while you’re on the line.

Step 2

We match the yacht

We recommend the right boat from our 9-vessel fleet for your group and budget — no platforms, no broker markup.

Step 3

Step aboard

Meet us at our Miami River dock — about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 from Miami Beach — and your captain takes it from there.

Every charter is 4 hours with a licensed captain and crew, the prices are the same on every date of the year, and we book in English and Spanish. See the FAQ for the rest.

Miami Yacht Collective is a premier yacht rental company based on the Miami River in Miami. We make luxury accessible — whether you’re planning a birthday celebration, bachelorette party, corporate outing, or a sunset cruise with friends. Every charter includes a licensed captain, fuel, ice, and a Bluetooth sound system.
