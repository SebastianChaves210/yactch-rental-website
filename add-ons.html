Included on every charter

  • The whole yacht, private to your group
  • Licensed captain and professional crew
  • 4 hours on the water from the Miami River dock
  • Children’s life jackets on board
  • Service in English and Spanish
  • The same price on every date of the year

Built into specific yachts

  • 48' Isabella with Jet Ski — a jet ski comes with the charter at no extra cost.

Have something specific in mind?
