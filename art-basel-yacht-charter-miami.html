Basel week on the water

For one week in December the whole art world is in Miami Beach, and the best seat in the city is on a boat. A private yacht gives your group a few hours away from the fair crowds, with the skyline, the islands and the sunset as the backdrop. Collectors use it to host clients, galleries use it to entertain, and groups of friends use it to turn an afternoon off into the best day of the trip.

We do not add an event surcharge. Art Basel week is priced like any other week: every charter is 4 hours with a licensed captain and crew included, from $1,000 on the 46' Maxum to $4,999 on the 90' Deep Blue with its jacuzzi.

Art Basel Miami Beach 2026 dates

  • VIP preview days: December 2 and 3, 2026.
  • Public days: December 4 to 6, 2026, at the Miami Beach Convention Center.
  • Our dock: 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 15 minutes from the Convention Center by car.

Boats book out around the fair, so the earlier you call, the more of the fleet is open on your date.

Three ways to do Basel on a yacht

  • Client hospitality: A corporate charter on the 65' Azimut L'chaim, 75' Ferretti or 90' Deep Blue: the aft deck for conversation, the flybridge for the view, and a dock 15 minutes from the fair.
  • The sunset escape: A late-afternoon slot so the last hour runs the skyline at sunset before the night's events.
  • The daytime reset: Star Island, Fisher Island and a swim at Nixon's Sandbar on the 48' Isabella or 62' Anvera, back in time for the evening openings.

Frequently asked questions

Are prices higher during Art Basel?

No. Our prices are the same every week of the year: from $1,000 for 4 hours with captain and crew included.

How far is the dock from the Miami Beach Convention Center?

About 15 minutes by car. Our dock is at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River.

Can we host clients on board?

Yes. The 65' Azimut L'chaim, 75' Ferretti and 90' Deep Blue all have an aft deck with a table and a flybridge with a view, and the crew looks after your guests.

When should we book for Basel week?

As early as you can. The fair draws a crowd that books boats, and the first weekend of December fills first.
