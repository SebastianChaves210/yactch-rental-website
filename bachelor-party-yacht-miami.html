Start the weekend on the water

Miami bachelor parties tend to follow the same script: land, dinner, a late night. The boat day is what breaks it up. Four hours on Biscayne Bay with the whole group on one deck, nobody driving, and a captain and crew running the boat.

It is a private charter. The only people on board are the ones in the group chat.

Three ways groups run it

  • The jet ski day. The 48' Isabella comes with a jet ski included. Anchor in the bay and take turns while the rest of the group holds down the flybridge. $1,499 for 4 hours.
  • The fast boat. The 28' Axopar Brabus is a twin-outboard sport boat that gets to the sandbar quickly and looks the part doing it. $1,200 for 4 hours.
  • The big yacht. The 65' Azimut L'chaim and 75' Ferretti at $3,000, or the 90' Deep Blue with Jacuzzi at $4,999, when the groom deserves the full production.

The route

Board at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 from Miami Beach. Cruise downriver under the bridges, out past the Brickell towers, and across the bay past Star Island and Fisher Island. Anchor at Nixon's Sandbar for the swim, then ride back with the skyline in front of you. Leaving in the afternoon puts the return leg at golden hour, in time for dinner.
