Do You Need a License to Rent a Boat in Miami?

Short answer: not if your charter comes with a captain. If you plan to drive the boat yourself, Florida law may require a safety card. Here is exactly who needs what.

On a captained charter
No license needed
Driving it yourself
Safety card if born 1988 or later
Jet skis
Same card rule applies

The short answer

Florida does not issue a boating license. What it has is the Boating Safety Education Identification Card, and whether you need one depends on a single question: who is driving?

  • A licensed captain drives: nobody in your group needs a card, a license or any experience. This is how every Miami Yacht Collective charter runs.
  • You drive: if you were born on or after January 1, 1988, Florida requires you to carry the card and a photo ID to operate any boat with an engine of 10 horsepower or more.

Who needs the Boating Safety Education ID Card

The rule is set by the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC). It applies to the person operating the boat, not to passengers, and it applies to visitors as well as Florida residents.

Your situationWhat you need
Guest on a captained charterNothing
Driving a rental boat, born before January 1, 1988No card required
Driving a rental boat, born on or after January 1, 1988Boating Safety Education ID Card (or temporary certificate) and photo ID
Visiting from another state with a boating safety cardA card from a NASBLA-approved course is accepted in Florida

To get the card you complete an FWC-approved boating safety course and pass its exam. The card does not expire.

The temporary certificate for visitors

If you are in Miami for a few days and want to drive a rental boat yourself, there is a faster route: the Temporary Boating Safety Certificate. You take a short test, online or through an approved vendor, and the certificate is valid for 90 days. Most self-drive rental operators in Miami will point you to it before they hand over the keys.

Jet skis follow the same rule

A jet ski is a boat under Florida law, so the same card requirement applies to anyone born in 1988 or later. There are age limits on top of that: you must be at least 14 to operate a personal watercraft in Florida and at least 18 to rent one.

Frequently asked questions

Does Florida have a boating license?

No. Florida issues a Boating Safety Education Identification Card instead. Anyone born on or after January 1, 1988 must carry it, with photo ID, to operate a boat with 10 horsepower or more.

Do I need a license for a captained yacht charter in Miami?

No. When a licensed captain operates the boat, guests need no license, card or boating experience. Every Miami Yacht Collective charter includes a licensed captain and crew.

Can a tourist drive a rental boat in Miami?

Yes. Visitors born on or after January 1, 1988 need either a boating safety card from a NASBLA-approved course or a Florida Temporary Boating Safety Certificate, which is valid for 90 days.

How old do you have to be to drive a jet ski in Florida?

At least 14 to operate a personal watercraft and at least 18 to rent one. Operators born on or after January 1, 1988 also need the boating safety card or a temporary certificate.
