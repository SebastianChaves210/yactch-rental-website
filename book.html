Frequently asked questions

How fast do you confirm?

Most charters are confirmed in under 10 minutes by phone or WhatsApp. We check availability live, match the yacht to your group and hold the date before the call ends.

Do I pay on the website?

No. The site takes no payments. Once the charter is confirmed we send you the payment details directly.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Give us the name and phone number of the person who will be on board so the crew can reach them on the day.
