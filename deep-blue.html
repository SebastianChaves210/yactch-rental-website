A 90-foot superyacht with a Jacuzzi on board

Deep Blue is the headline yacht of the fleet: 90 feet, an onboard Jacuzzi, a licensed captain and crew, from $4,999 for 4 hours. If the brief is “the biggest, best yacht day Miami can offer,” this is the answer.

A Jacuzzi on the water changes the whole charter — anchor off Star Island or in the bay, and the deck becomes the party. Deep Blue is the yacht booked for the events that only happen once: the big birthday, the bachelorette weekend, the company celebration, the proposal.

It's one of two 90-footers we run; the 90' Acgua Alberti is the same length at $5,000. If 90 feet is more yacht than you need, the 75' Ferretti is $3,000. Departures are from 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, 10 minutes from Brickell. See how a yacht party or bachelorette charter runs at this level.

On board Deep Blue

You board onto a wide teak aft deck with a full-width sofa and stairs up to the flybridge. Glass doors open into the main salon: two cream sofas, glossy wood finishes and long windows down both sides.

Forward of the salon, a formal dining table sits next to a galley with a stone-topped island and a full-size refrigerator, and beyond that is the helm with its twin captain's chairs. Below deck are a master cabin with a double bed, twin-bed guest cabins and bathrooms. The Jacuzzi is what sets Deep Blue apart from every other yacht in the fleet.
