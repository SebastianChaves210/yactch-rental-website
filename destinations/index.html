The route, from the dock out

  1. StartThe Miami River

    Board at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. The first ten minutes run under the bridges and past the Brickell towers.

  2. BayBiscayne Bay and the skyline

    The river opens into the bay with downtown Miami and Brickell behind you — the view every photo of Miami is trying to get.

  3. IslandsStar, Palm, Hibiscus and the Venetian Islands

    Waterfront estates behind guarded gates, best seen exactly the way we see them: from the water, at a slow pace.

  4. CutFisher Island and Government Cut

    The private island at the mouth of the shipping channel, reachable only by boat or ferry, with South Beach across the water.

  5. SwimNixon’s Sandbar

    A shallow stretch off Key Biscayne where you stand in the water beside the boat. The stop on almost every party and birthday charter we run.

  6. Far endStiltsville

    Wooden houses standing on the flats at the edge of the bay inside Biscayne National Park, with the skyline on the horizon behind you.

Comparing the swim spots? The Miami sandbar guide covers Nixon’s, Haulover and Stiltsville side by side, and the best time to charter covers seasons, sunset times and weekday versus weekend.

Frequently asked questions

Where do charters depart from?

From our dock at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach, with easy parking and no marina crowds. Every charter starts on the river and heads into Biscayne Bay.

Where can the yacht take us in four hours?

Classic routes cover Biscayne Bay, the Star Island and Fisher Island mansions, swim stops at Nixon's Sandbar, day trips to Stiltsville and sunset skyline cruises. Your captain shapes the route around your group.

Can you take us to Stiltsville?

Yes. Stiltsville sits on the flats at the edge of Biscayne Bay inside Biscayne National Park. Tell us when you book and we'll plan a charter long enough to include it.

Can you pick us up in Miami Beach or Brickell?

Every charter boards at our Miami River dock, which is about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 from Miami Beach. The location pages explain the easiest way to reach it from each neighborhood.
