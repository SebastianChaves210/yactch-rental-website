The meeting nobody skips

A conference room gets you attendance. A private yacht on Biscayne Bay gets you attention. A corporate yacht charter puts your clients or your team on their own boat for the afternoon — licensed captain and crew included — with the Miami skyline, Star Island and open water doing the work a slide deck can't.

Every charter is fully private. There are no other groups on board and no fixed timetable, so the day runs to your agenda: a relaxed client cruise, a reward day for a team that hit its number, a product or brand shoot, or the closing event of a Miami conference week.

What companies book us for

  • Client entertainment: Four uninterrupted hours with the people you most need time with, in a setting they'll talk about afterwards.
  • Team outings and offsites: Get everyone out of the office and onto the bay — a sandbar swim does more for a team than another dinner.
  • Company celebrations: Milestones, closings, launches and year-end parties on a yacht sized to the occasion.
  • Conference and convention groups: In Miami for an event? Our dock is about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 from Miami Beach.
  • Photo and content shoots: Yachts from a 28-foot Brabus sport boat to a 90-foot superyacht as the backdrop.

How to plan it

Step 1

Call or WhatsApp

Tell us your date, group size and the occasion. We answer 24/7 and check live availability while you’re on the line.

Step 2

We match the yacht

We recommend the right boat from our 9-vessel fleet for your group and budget — no platforms, no broker markup.

Step 3

Step aboard

Meet us at our Miami River dock — about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 from Miami Beach — and your captain takes it from there.

A corporate charter, hour by hour

  • Hour 1: Board at our Miami River dock and cruise downriver as the Brickell skyline opens up.
  • Hour 2: Out into Biscayne Bay, past Star Island and Fisher Island — the stretch where the phones come out.
  • Hour 3: Anchor in the bay. Swim, talk, or simply let the group settle in on deck.
  • Hour 4: Ride back toward the city through golden hour.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a corporate yacht charter in Miami cost?

From $1,000 for 4 hours with a licensed captain and crew included. Most corporate groups book the 65' Azimut L'chaim or 75' Ferretti at $3,000, or the 90-foot Deep Blue at $4,999 for 4 hours.

Which yacht is best for a corporate event?

For flagship events, the 90' Deep Blue with Jacuzzi. For client entertainment and team outings, the 75' Ferretti and 65' Azimut L'chaim. Tell us the group and the brief and we'll recommend the right yacht.

Where do corporate charters depart from?

From our dock at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach.

How far ahead should we book?

Call or WhatsApp (787) 664-5040 as soon as you have a date. We check live availability while you're on the line and can hold the yacht the same day.
