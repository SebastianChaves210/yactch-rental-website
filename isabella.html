The yacht that comes with a jet ski

The Isabella 48' is the yacht in our fleet that comes with a jet ski included in the charter. For $1,499 you get a 48-foot motor yacht, a licensed captain and crew, and a jet ski to take turns on once the anchor is down.

That combination makes it the most fun-per-dollar boat we run. Cruise out past the skyline, drop anchor in Biscayne Bay, and half the group is on the water while the other half is on deck. It's the yacht we point people to for birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette days, and any group that gets restless just sitting still.

Charters leave from 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell. If you don't need the jet ski, the 46' Maxum starts at $1,000; if you want more yacht, the 55' Azimut is next in line. How booking works

Call or WhatsApp (787) 664-5040, any hour. We check the Isabella's availability for your date while you're on the line, confirm the rate, and hold the charter — most bookings are done in under 10 minutes. No platforms, no booking fees, and you deal directly with the team running the boat.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Isabella cost to rent in Miami?

$1,499 for a 4-hour private charter, licensed captain and crew included. Call or WhatsApp (787) 664-5040 for an exact quote for your date.

What is the Isabella best for?

Birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette days, and active groups. The charter comes with a jet ski included.

Do I need a boating license?

No. Every Miami Yacht Collective charter comes with a licensed captain and crew, so nobody in your group needs a license or any boating experience.

Where does the Isabella depart from?

From our dock at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. From there you cruise down the river into Biscayne Bay.
