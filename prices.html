Yacht rentals in Miami under $2,000

You don't need superyacht money for a real yacht day. Six of our nine boats start under $2,000 for 4 hours: the 46' Maxum at $1,000 is the best-value full-size cruiser in the fleet; the 25' Yamaha 255XD ($1,000) and 28' Axopar Brabus ($1,200) suit smaller groups who want speed and watersports; the 48' Isabella ($1,499) includes a jet ski; the 55' Azimut ($1,600) is the most affordable way into a true luxury yacht; and the 62' Anvera ($1,700) brings Italian-built performance in under two grand.

What actually moves the price

  • Yacht size and class. The single biggest factor. A 90-foot superyacht with a jacuzzi costs more to run than a 46-foot cruiser — so pay for the space and experience you want, not headcount.
  • Charter length. Rates above are for 4 hours. Longer days scale up — ask for the multi-hour quote when you book.
  • Date and season. Peak weekends, holidays and event weeks in Miami book out first. Same-day and weekday charters are often the easiest to schedule.
  • Extras. Some boats bundle features others don't — the Isabella includes a jet ski, the Deep Blue has an on-deck jacuzzi. Tell us what your group wants and we'll match the boat instead of upselling the biggest one.

Booking direct vs. marketplace platforms

Marketplace listings for Miami yachts often quote a low hourly rate, then add service fees, captain fees and fuel at checkout. Our prices work the other way: you talk to the operator directly, the captain and crew are already in the number, and the quote you get on the phone is the deal. It's also why we can confirm a charter in minutes instead of days of app messaging.

Pricing questions, answered

What is the cheapest yacht rental in Miami Yacht Collective's fleet?

The 46' Maxum at $1,000 for 4 hours — a spacious open-deck cruiser. The 25' Yamaha 255XD jet boat is also $1,000, and the 28' Axopar Brabus is $1,200 for 4 hours.

Do the prices include a captain and crew?

Yes. Every listed price includes a licensed captain and professional crew. You never drive the boat yourself and you don't need any boating license.

Why do prices differ so much between yachts?

Size and vessel class drive the rate. A higher price buys more deck space, more luxury and features like the Deep Blue's on-deck jacuzzi or Isabella's included jet ski — not more people.

Are there hidden fees or platform markups?

You book direct with us by phone or WhatsApp — no marketplace platform fees and no broker markup. Confirm the all-in total for your date when you book.
