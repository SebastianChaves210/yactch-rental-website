Who we are

Miami Yacht Collective arranges private yacht charters departing from 668 NW N River Dr, Miami, FL 33136. This policy explains what information we collect when you use miamiyachtcollective.com or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us. When you call, WhatsApp or email us you share your name, phone number and the details of the charter you want (date, group size, occasion). We use them to answer you and to arrange the charter.
  • Site analytics. The site uses Google Analytics 4 and Vercel Web Analytics to understand which pages are visited and which buttons are tapped (for example, a tap on the WhatsApp button). This data is aggregated and does not identify you by name.
  • Payments. The site itself takes no payments. If we send you a payment link, it is processed by Stripe under Stripe’s own privacy policy; we never see your full card number.

Cookies

Analytics cookies are set by Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics. The site sets no advertising cookies of its own. You can block cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to call or message us.

How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiry and arrange, confirm and run your charter.
  • To contact you about your booking (changes, weather, meeting point).
  • To improve the website and our service.

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the people needed to run your charter (for example, the captain of the yacht you booked) and with the service providers above.

How long we keep it

Booking details are kept for as long as needed to run the charter and meet our legal and accounting obligations, then deleted. [TODO(owner): confirm the retention period.]

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by contacting us at the details below. If you are in the EU, UK or a US state with privacy legislation, you may have additional rights under that law.

Third-party links

The site links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies.
