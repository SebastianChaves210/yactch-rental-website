Your spring break boat, not a ticket

Every March the party boats in Miami sell tickets by the hundred. A private charter is a different day: one yacht, your group, your playlist on the speakers, a swim stop at the sandbar and a licensed captain and crew who handle everything else. You split the cost of the boat across the group rather than paying per head, and nobody you did not invite is on board.

Same price as any other week. Every charter is 4 hours with captain and crew included, from $1,000 on the 46' Maxum or 25' Yamaha 255XD to $4,999 on the 90' Deep Blue.

The March calendar

  • Spring break: College weeks run through March, with the first half the busiest on the water.
  • Miami Music Week: March 22 to 28, 2027, the week of pool parties and club nights that surrounds Ultra.
  • Ultra Music Festival: March 26 to 28, 2027, at Bayfront Park on the downtown waterfront. A daytime charter before the festival gates open is the classic move.

Weekend boats in March book out early. Call as soon as your dates are set.

Step aboard

A spring break charter, hour by hour

  • Hour 1: Board on the Miami River and cruise out past the Brickell skyline into Biscayne Bay.
  • Hour 2: Star Island and Fisher Island on the way south.
  • Hour 3: Anchor at Nixon's Sandbar. Swim, float, music up. On the Isabella the jet ski comes out here.
  • Hour 4: The ride home with the skyline ahead, back at the dock in time for the night.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a spring break yacht in Miami?

From $1,000 for 4 hours with captain and crew included, split however your group likes. The 48' Isabella with a jet ski is $1,499 and the 65' Azimut L'chaim is $3,000 for 4 hours.

Do you raise prices for Ultra or Music Week?

No. Prices are the same every week of the year.

Can we bring our own music?

Yes. Bring your playlist. Every charter is private, so the speakers are yours for the 4 hours.

Can we swim?

Yes. Nixon's Sandbar off Key Biscayne is the swim stop on most March charters, and the captain picks the spot for the day's wind and tide.

Do we need a license?

No. A licensed captain and crew run every charter.
