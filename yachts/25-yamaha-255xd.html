The fleet's pocket rocket

The Yamaha 255XD is the smallest, quickest boat in our fleet — a 25-foot jet boat built for groups who want speed, spray and sandbar time rather than a floating lounge. Jet propulsion means shallow-water agility, so your captain can tuck into spots on Biscayne Bay that the big yachts pass by.

It's the right call for a fast skyline tour, a watersports afternoon, or an add-on chase boat alongside a larger charter from the fleet. Want more deck for the same group? The 28' Axopar Brabus and 46' Maxum are the next steps up.

A sample 4-hour charter on the Yamaha 255XD

Every charter is private, so this is a starting point rather than a schedule. Tell your captain what matters most — the swim stop, the skyline, the sunset — and the day bends around it.

  1. 0:00Board on the Miami River

    Meet your captain and crew at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. Welcome aboard, a short safety briefing, music on.

  2. 0:15Down the river into the bay

    Under the bridges and past the Brickell towers into Biscayne Bay. The skyline opens up behind you.

  3. 1:00The islands loop

    Run the bay at speed past Star Island and Fisher Island — the ride itself is half the point on this boat.

  4. 1:45Anchor and swim

    Jet propulsion means your captain can tuck into shallow sandbar spots the big yachts pass by. Nixon's Sandbar off Key Biscayne is the classic stop; your captain picks the spot for the day's wind and tide.

  5. 3:15The slow ride home

    Back along the skyline with the city lighting up. Book the late slot and this leg is your sunset cruise.

  6. 4:00Back at the dock

    Four hours on the water, door to door from the Miami River.
