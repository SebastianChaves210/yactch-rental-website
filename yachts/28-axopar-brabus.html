A Brabus on the water

The 28' Axopar Brabus Edition is the sport boat of the fleet — a 28-foot Axopar finished by Brabus, built for performance and style. It charters with a licensed captain and crew for $1,200 for 4 hours.

This isn't a lounge-and-drift yacht. It's a fast, sharp-handling day boat with bold Brabus design, made for running the bay, hopping between spots and getting to the sandbar before everyone else. It's the pick for small groups, couples and anyone who'd take a supercar over a limo.

For a similar fast-boat day at $1,000, see the 25' Yamaha 255XD; for a proper yacht deck, the 48' Isabella comes with a jet ski at $1,499. On board the Axopar

The Axopar is a walkaround sport boat: you move from bow to stern along either side without climbing over anything. The helm sits under a T-top with rows of diamond-stitched navy seats behind it, and the aft deck is open for sun and for getting in and out of the water.

Twin outboards on the transom make it one of the fastest rides in the fleet, so more of your four hours go to the stop itself.

A sample 4-hour charter on the Axopar Brabus

Every charter is private, so this is a starting point rather than a schedule. Tell your captain what matters most — the swim stop, the skyline, the sunset — and the day bends around it.

  1. 0:00Board on the Miami River

    Meet your captain and crew at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. Welcome aboard, a short safety briefing, music on.

  2. 0:15Down the river into the bay

    Under the bridges and past the Brickell towers into Biscayne Bay. The skyline opens up behind you.

  3. 1:00The islands loop

    Run the bay at speed past Star Island and Fisher Island — the ride itself is half the point on this boat.

  4. 1:45Anchor and swim

    Get to the sandbar before everyone else; the open aft deck is the way in and out of the water. Nixon's Sandbar off Key Biscayne is the classic stop; your captain picks the spot for the day's wind and tide.

  5. 3:15The slow ride home

    Back along the skyline with the city lighting up. Book the late slot and this leg is your sunset cruise.

  6. 4:00Back at the dock

    Four hours on the water, door to door from the Miami River.
