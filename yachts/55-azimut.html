Italian lines, mid-size price

The 55' Azimut is the sweet spot of the fleet: a 55-foot Italian motor yacht that looks every bit the part in photos and still comes in at $1,600 for 4 hours with a licensed captain and crew.

Azimut is one of the best-known names in Italian yacht building, and this one is built to cruise — sleek profile, strong performance, real yacht presence when you pull up to the sandbar. It's a natural pick for bachelorette groups, milestone birthdays and anyone who wants a step up from an entry boat without going to superyacht rates.

Compare it with the 62' Anvera at $1,700 if you want a sportier feel, or move up to the 65' Azimut L'chaim for more yacht. Charters depart the Miami River and cruise Biscayne Bay, Star Island and Fisher Island; a full day can reach Stiltsville or Nixon's Sandbar. Planning a group event? A sample 4-hour charter on the 55' Azimut

Every charter is private, so this is a starting point rather than a schedule. Tell your captain what matters most — the swim stop, the skyline, the sunset — and the day bends around it.

  1. 0:00Board on the Miami River

    Meet your captain and crew at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. Welcome aboard, a short safety briefing, music on.

  2. 0:15Down the river into the bay

    Under the bridges and past the Brickell towers into Biscayne Bay. The skyline opens up behind you.

  3. 1:00The islands loop

    Loop Star Island, Palm and Hibiscus Islands and run down past Fisher Island with the skyline behind you.

  4. 1:45Anchor and swim

    Anchor in the bay and use the full-width swim platform to get in. Nixon's Sandbar off Key Biscayne is the classic stop; your captain picks the spot for the day's wind and tide.

  5. 3:15The slow ride home

    Back along the skyline with the city lighting up. Book the late slot and this leg is your sunset cruise.

  6. 4:00Back at the dock

    Four hours on the water, door to door from the Miami River.
