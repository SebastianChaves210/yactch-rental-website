The sport yacht of the fleet

The 62' Anvera is the yacht for people who care how a boat moves. It's a 62-foot high-performance Italian yacht — low, sleek and fast — chartered with a licensed captain and crew from $1,700 for 4 hours.

Where most yachts this size are built like floating apartments, the Anvera is built like a sports car: clean open deck, sharp lines, and a ride that makes the run across Biscayne Bay part of the day rather than the commute. It photographs better than anything near its price, which is why it's a favorite for content shoots, couples and sunset charters.

Departures are from our Miami River dock, 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 from Miami Beach. Want a more classic yacht layout at a similar rate? Look at the 55' Azimut. Want more length? The 65' Azimut L'chaim and 75' Ferretti are both $3,000. The Anvera is also our top pick for a private sunset cruise.

On board the Anvera

The Anvera is built around one long open deck. At the stern are three wide sun loungers and terraces that fold down to open the deck out over the water. Midship, a dining table and bench seating sit in the shade of the hardtop, with speakers built into the seating.

Forward of the helm, a sunpad covers the bow. Below deck is a bright white cabin with a wraparound lounge and a sofa: a place to get out of the sun without leaving the party.

A sample 4-hour charter on the Anvera

Every charter is private, so this is a starting point rather than a schedule. Tell your captain what matters most — the swim stop, the skyline, the sunset — and the day bends around it.

  1. 0:00Board on the Miami River

    Meet your captain and crew at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. Welcome aboard, a short safety briefing, music on.

  2. 0:15Down the river into the bay

    Under the bridges and past the Brickell towers into Biscayne Bay. The skyline opens up behind you.

  3. 1:00The islands loop

    Loop Star Island, Palm and Hibiscus Islands and run down past Fisher Island with the skyline behind you.

  4. 1:45Anchor and swim

    Fold the terraces down at anchor and the deck opens out over the water. Nixon's Sandbar off Key Biscayne is the classic stop; your captain picks the spot for the day's wind and tide.

  5. 3:15The slow ride home

    Back along the skyline with the city lighting up. Book the late slot and this leg is your sunset cruise.

  6. 4:00Back at the dock

    Four hours on the water, door to door from the Miami River.
