A 65-foot Azimut built for celebrations

L'chaim means “to life” — a toast — and this 65-foot Azimut is chartered exactly that way. It's a full-size Italian motor yacht with a licensed captain and crew, from $3,000 for 4 hours.

At 65 feet you're in proper big-yacht territory: the kind of boat that turns heads coming down the Miami River and holds its own anchored off Star Island. It's the yacht we recommend when the occasion matters — a milestone birthday, an engagement, a bachelorette weekend's main event, or a client day that has to land.

It sits between the 62' Anvera at $1,700 and the 90-foot Deep Blue at $4,999, and shares its rate with the 75' Ferretti. Charters depart 668 NW N River Dr and cruise Biscayne Bay, Star Island and Fisher Island, with Stiltsville and Nixon's Sandbar in reach on longer bookings. Planning the party? On board the L'chaim

The L'chaim gives a group three places to be at once. The flybridge has its own helm, a lounge with a table and a sunpad under the bimini. The aft deck has a teak dining table in the shade, and the bow has a wide sunpad.

Inside, the salon is finished in tan leather and cherry wood, with a galley alongside. Below are a master cabin, a VIP cabin, a twin cabin and bathrooms with showers. The inflatable slide and water toys shown in the gallery are available at an additional cost.

A sample 4-hour charter on the L'chaim

Every charter is private, so this is a starting point rather than a schedule. Tell your captain what matters most — the swim stop, the skyline, the sunset — and the day bends around it.

  1. 0:00Board on the Miami River

    Meet your captain and crew at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. Welcome aboard, a short safety briefing, music on.

  2. 0:15Down the river into the bay

    Under the bridges and past the Brickell towers into Biscayne Bay. The skyline opens up behind you.

  3. 1:00The islands loop

    Loop Star Island, Palm and Hibiscus Islands and run down past Fisher Island with the skyline behind you.

  4. 1:45Anchor and swim

    Anchor and swim; the inflatable slide and water toys can be added to the charter. Nixon's Sandbar off Key Biscayne is the classic stop; your captain picks the spot for the day's wind and tide.

  5. 3:15The slow ride home

    Back along the skyline with the city lighting up. Book the late slot and this leg is your sunset cruise.

  6. 4:00Back at the dock

    Four hours on the water, door to door from the Miami River.
