On board Deep Blue

You board onto a wide teak aft deck with a full-width sofa and stairs up to the flybridge. Glass doors open into the main salon: two cream sofas, glossy wood finishes and long windows down both sides.

Forward of the salon, a formal dining table sits next to a galley with a stone-topped island and a full-size refrigerator, and beyond that is the helm with its twin captain's chairs. Below deck are a master cabin with a double bed, twin-bed guest cabins and bathrooms. The Jacuzzi is what sets Deep Blue apart from every other yacht in the fleet.

A sample 4-hour charter on the Deep Blue

Every charter is private, so this is a starting point rather than a schedule. Tell your captain what matters most — the swim stop, the skyline, the sunset — and the day bends around it.

  1. 0:00Board on the Miami River

    Meet your captain and crew at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. Welcome aboard, a short safety briefing, music on.

  2. 0:15Down the river into the bay

    Under the bridges and past the Brickell towers into Biscayne Bay. The skyline opens up behind you.

  3. 1:00The islands loop

    Loop Star Island, Palm and Hibiscus Islands and run down past Fisher Island with the skyline behind you.

  4. 1:45Anchor and swim

    Anchor off Star Island or in the bay and the Jacuzzi becomes the party. Nixon's Sandbar off Key Biscayne is the classic stop; your captain picks the spot for the day's wind and tide.

  5. 3:15The slow ride home

    Back along the skyline with the city lighting up. Book the late slot and this leg is your sunset cruise.

  6. 4:00Back at the dock

    Four hours on the water, door to door from the Miami River.
