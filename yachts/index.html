How to choose

Fast and small. The 25' Yamaha 255XD and the 28' Axopar Brabus Edition are day boats for couples and small groups who want speed, spray and shallow sandbar spots the big yachts pass by.

The classic yacht day. The 46' Maxum is the lowest-priced yacht in the fleet, the 48' Isabella brings a jet ski along, and the 55' Azimut and 62' Anvera add Italian lines and a sportier ride. These are the boats most birthdays, bachelorettes and sunset cruises book.

When the occasion matters. The 65' Azimut L'chaim and the 75' Ferretti give a group three decks and real yacht presence at $3,000. The 90' Deep Blue with Jacuzzi is the headline boat for corporate events, big birthdays and once-only celebrations.

Not sure? Tell us the date, the group and the occasion on WhatsApp or by phone and we'll recommend the boat. Browse by occasion on the experiences pages or by where you want to go on the destinations pages.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a yacht in Miami?

Our private charters start at $1,000 for 4 hours on the 46' Maxum or the 25' Yamaha 255XD. Mid-size 55–62 ft yachts run $1,600–$1,700 for 4 hours, 65–75 ft yachts are $3,000, and the 90-foot Deep Blue with Jacuzzi is $4,999 for 4 hours — captain and crew always included. See the full price guide.

What's included in the price?

Every charter includes the yacht, a licensed captain and professional crew. You never drive the boat yourself, and nobody in your group needs a boating license.

Which yacht is right for my group?

Small, fast groups take the 25' Yamaha 255XD or the 28' Axopar Brabus. Birthdays and bachelorettes usually land on the 46' Maxum, the 48' Isabella with Jet Ski or the 55' Azimut. Milestone celebrations and client days go to the 62' Anvera, the 65' Azimut L'chaim and the 75' Ferretti, and once-only events to the 90' Deep Blue. Tell us the occasion and we'll match the boat.

Where do charters depart from?

From our dock at 668 NW N River Dr on the Miami River, about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 minutes from Miami Beach. From there you cruise down the river into Biscayne Bay.
